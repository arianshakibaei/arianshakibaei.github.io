About

Mr. Shakibaei is an analyst with Deloitte's Government and Public Services practice. His work involves grants management programs covering the grant lifecycle, serving federal health and state agencies.

Mr. Shakibaei received a B.S. in Finance with a summa cum laude distinction from the Pennsylvania State University.
